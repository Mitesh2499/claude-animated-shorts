## Caption

Ben Stokes has registered for the IPL 2027 auction at the top base price of ₹2 crore. 🚨

He's one of 1,220 players chasing just 66 slots, and only 25 of those are for overseas players. That's roughly 11 overseas names for every overseas spot, and franchises still have to shortlist before the final list on 4 December.

His IPL record: 45 matches, 935 runs, 28 wickets, and a ₹16.25 crore price tag from CSK in 2023. But he hasn't played an IPL game since, and he retired from international cricket in June 2026.
