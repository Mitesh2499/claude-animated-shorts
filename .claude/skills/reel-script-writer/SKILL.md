# Reel script writer (analyse -> understand WHY -> write something original)

Study material: `references/reel-script/RAW_STORY_ANALYSIS.md` (the 7-point analysis of the Raw video, done already) and `references/illustrated/STYLE_BIBLE_ILLUSTRATED.md` (measured camera, colour, sound).
Output template: `templates/reel-script/SCRIPT_TEMPLATE.md`. Worked original example: `templates/reel-script/EXAMPLE_original.md`.
Hand-off: the script's shot table becomes `story.ts` for `illustrated-parallax-short` (or the shot plan of `cartoon-3d-short`).

## Two learned patterns: choose first
| | Pattern A "karma justice" | Pattern B "sage parable" |

| Source | Krishna short in Raw/ (`RAW_STORY_ANALYSIS.md`) | `Raw/Video-43709.mp4` (`VIDEO43709_ANALYSIS.md`, Marathi script supplied by the user) |

Pick by the user's topic; if unclear, ask one question. Pattern B beat sheet, language rules and checklist: `templates/reel-script/PATTERN_B_sage_parable.md`. Everything below applies to both unless it says "A".

## Language
Language = what the user asks; otherwise match the pasted sample (Marathi script -> Marathi). Marathi and Hindi both use Devanagari; always add Roman transliteration + a one-line English gloss. Marathi: use real Marathi forms ("आहे", "होतं", "नका", "पाठीशी"), not Hindi words in Devanagari. Marathi/Hindi narration runs ~1.7-2.2 words/s, so a 75 s Pattern B is ~150-190 words.

## Hard rules
- **Original only.** Never reuse the sample's story (beggar's bowl kicked, smoking man with sunglasses, vishwaroop, choke), dialogue, characters or watermark. Reuse the PRINCIPLES.
- The sample is private study material: never quote it publicly or name its creator in a script.
- Hindi: Devanagari for every spoken line, plus a one-line Roman transliteration under it for the voice artist/TTS. Ordinary characters speak conversational Hindi (short sentences, everyday words: "यार", "भाई", "अरे", "चल", "सुन", mild Hinglish where natural). Krishna and divine beings speak shuddh, poetic, slow Hindi (Sanskrit-rooted words, rhythm, calm second person "तू"/"तुम", never slang, never shouting). Never mock any deity or faith; the divine figure is the moral centre.
- Violence is stylised. Punishment is humiliation, loss, or realisation, not gore. Never cruelty to children or animals shown in detail.
- Captions: Pattern A none unless asked; Pattern B captions ON (the sample uses them). In either case the story should be understandable with sound off.
- The user's pasted sample scripts are study material: never reuse their characters (Anvi, Omkar, Nikhil), proverbs-with-context, boat metaphor, Punarvasu pun or the closing line. Reuse the technique (refrain, triple parallel, metaphor-for-advice, wisdom beat, "found in the first step" reframe style).
- Facts/jokes: any "real fact" used must be true and sourced in a note at the top.
- 30-45 s, ~2.2-2.8 Hindi words/s spoken (Hindi runs slower than English, 80-110 words total including all characters). Fewer words is better: the sample is mostly told with pictures.

## Step 1: Analyse the sample (always do all 7, 2-4 lines each, tie each to a timestamp)
1. **Hook:** what is in frame 1 and what question or emotion does it plant (the scroll-stop trigger)? Is there a moral wound (unfairness, pity) before any explanation?
2. **Structure/pacing/transitions:** shot lengths, where the pace speeds up, which cuts are hard vs dip.
3. **Emotional triggers + relatability:** whose pain does the viewer share, which everyday behaviour does it indict, which viewer thinks "that is me / I have seen this".
4. **Conversations, expressions, pauses:** who speaks, who is silent, where silence does the work, how faces change (held sad face, grin -> grit -> break).
5. **Suspense/curiosity loops/revelations:** the open loop (who is watching?), the delayed payoff, the reveal image.
6. **Cinematography, music, SFX:** push-ins, framing, palette per beat, ambient -> score jump, hit sounds, final silence.
7. **Ending + shareability:** the last image/line, why a viewer sends it to someone.
Then write **WHY it works** in 5 bullets (principles, not events). The analysis for the Raw video is already in `references/reel-script/RAW_STORY_ANALYSIS.md`; re-derive only for a NEW sample, and say if dialogue could not be transcribed (do not invent what was said).

## Step 2: Pick the original story
Choose in this order, state each in one line:
1. **Real-life struggle** the Indian audience knows (wages withheld, mother's medicine, exam fee, in-laws, a school-going child working, ignoring an old parent, showing off charity, honesty at a cost).
2. **Offender behaviour** that viewers recognise in themselves or others (pride, greed, ego, dishonesty) and ONE specific small cruel/foolish act that can be shown in one picture.
3. **The witness** (a silent victim/child/old person) and what they never say.
4. **The twist that changes meaning** (see below), not just "bad man gets punished".
5. **The memorable closing line** (6-12 words, poetic, quotable, shareable).
Twist menu (pick one, never more than one): the helper was the divine in disguise; the punished one is the one who gave to a temple publicly but denied a person; the victim's small kindness returns; what the offender threw away is exactly what he later needs; the divine asks the victim, not the offender, for the verdict; the offender sees the act replayed from the victim's eyes.

## Step 3: Write the script with this beat sheet (30-45 s)
| time | beat | job |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | HOOK | An unfair act already underway, victim's sad face visible, no setup, no title. Sound: one real-world SFX + low ambient. Stop-the-scroll = a wound + an open question. |
| 3-9 s | SETUP + ESCALATE | One more cruelty or one more person walks past; viewers feel anger/pity rising. Max 2 lines of dialogue. |
| 9-13 s | WITNESS | Quiet held close-up of the victim (tear, trembling hand). Music drops out. No dialogue or one whisper. |
| 13-16 s | TURN | Dip to dark / letterbox eyes / silence: something is watching. Curiosity loop opens ("kaun?"). |
| 16-19 s | OFFENDER BEAT | Offender smug for 2 s, face shown now, so the fall is bigger. |
| 19-27 s | REVEAL | Divine entrance, hero light, score enters (+10 dB). Krishna's lines: 2-3 poetic sentences. |
| 27-35 s | CONSEQUENCE | The truth lands: offender's own act is shown back to him, or loss, or terrified realisation. 1-2 short lines. |
| 35-41 s | TWIST + PAYOFF | The single twist, then the closing line. Black. Music tail 2-3 s. |
Cut a beat before cutting the hook. Every shot: one idea, one change in the picture.

## Step 4: Output format (use `templates/reel-script/SCRIPT_TEMPLATE.md`)
1. **Analysis** of the sample (7 points) + WHY it works (5 bullets).
2. **Concept**: title (for the post, not on screen), logline, moral, twist, share trigger, hashtags (5).
3. **Timestamped script table:** `time | shot # | scene description | camera | expression/acting | dialogue (Devanagari + Roman) | music | SFX`.
4. **Voice notes:** per character pace, pitch, emotion; divine voice direction (slow, warm, resonant, a half-beat pause before key words).
5. **Music/SFX cue sheet** (when each starts/stops, dB moves).
6. **Shot list for production:** ids, seconds, `Cam pct`, palette (warm vs cold), layers needed, maps 1:1 to `story.ts`.
7. **Self-check** (below).

## Self-check before delivering
- Hook visible in frame 1 and understood with sound off? Is the victim's face shown within 3 s?
- Is there exactly ONE twist and ONE quotable closing line? Would you send it to someone?
- Do the first 16 s carry no more than ~25 spoken words?
- Ordinary characters sound like real people; Krishna sounds like scripture, not a speech at a rally?
- Not a copy: no shared character, object, location, or line with the sample?
- Total words 80-110, runtime 30-45 s, every line spoken aloud once to check 2.2-2.8 words/s.
- No captions needed; no gore; no deity mocked; any fact true.

## Hand-off to production
Convert the shot list into `templates/illustrated/story.ts` plan rows (`[id, seconds, note]`), the layer list into `ASSETS.md`, the cue sheet into the `templates/audio/` event table. Voice: `edge-tts` Hindi voices (e.g. hi-IN-MadhurNeural for men, hi-IN-SwaraNeural for women/child) or the user's own recording; the divine voice slower by 10-15 % with reverb tail.
