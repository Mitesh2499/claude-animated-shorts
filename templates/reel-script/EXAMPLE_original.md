**Concept**
- Logline: A contractor refuses a labourer his wages, then donates a gold crown to Krishna in front of the whole temple.
- Moral: Wages you hold back weigh more than the gold you offer.
- Relatable: delayed wages, a child's school fees.
- Twist: the crown does not fit the idol; it fits the labourer's daughter's tiny head, and Krishna says whose it was.
- Closing line: "मंदिर में मुकुट चढ़ाने से पहले, मज़दूर की मज़दूरी चढ़ा देना।" (Mandir mein mukut chadhane se pehle, mazdoor ki mazdoori chadha dena.)
