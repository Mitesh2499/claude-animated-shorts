## Visual system (measured)
- Background flat light grey **#f0f0f0 / #f2f2f2** (71 % of pixels, mean luma 229), faint dotted grid only on hook/CTA/logo beats. Text near-black **#1f1f1f**, secondary grey **#525252**, unspoken/just-spoken words light grey **#bcb9b8**, accent coral **#cc6e5a** (hook only, matches the Claude brand colour).
- Type: clean geometric sans (Inter/SF-like). Hierarchy per block: lead-in line regular ~4.5 % of height-equivalent (≈ 30 px at 720w), key phrase **bold ~1.5x**, punch numbers ("5 tools") ~2.5x bold, italics for the audience name ("*Claude Code Users*"), black bar with white bold text for the sting ("do not even know exist", "you need right now").
- Karaoke reveal: each word appears when spoken, first in light grey (slightly lower, smaller), then settles to dark in ~6-8 frames. Lines centre-aligned at the top; bullets left-aligned with a 4-point sparkle ✦ icon.
- Cards: screen recordings/screenshots in a rounded rectangle (r ≈ 2 % width) with a soft drop shadow, ~75 % frame width, slight 3D tilt and slow float/push (frame diff 1-4 per frame between word reveals = continuous gentle motion).
- Badge pill: white, rounded, shadow, square logo tile + tool NAME in bold caps, enters from the right, exits left on the cut.
- Watermark: brand URL bottom-centre at ~86 % height, light grey bold, always on.
- Layout zones (1080x1920): header 3-15 %, card 17-50 %, sub-line + bullets 50-80 %, watermark 86 %. Nothing in the bottom 12 % except the watermark (platform UI).

## Why it works
1. **Hook = qualifier + number + urgency** in 4 s ("If you use X, these are the N Y you need right now"): self-selects the audience and promises a finite payoff.
2. **Strict repeating template** (ordinal -> name badge -> what it does -> 3 concrete bullets) makes every item scannable and keeps people waiting for the next number.
3. **Proof on screen**: every claim sits on top of a real screen recording; text and visuals say the same thing at the same time (works muted).
4. **Words are the edit**: no b-roll hunting, every word is an event, so the screen changes 3x per second, which keeps retention high.
5. **Recap flatters the viewer** ("most users do not even know exist") right before the ask, then a related, more valuable promise (make it sound human) bridges to a comment-keyword lead magnet, gated by follow. The comment keyword boosts engagement signals; the follow gate converts.

## Weak spots to beat
- 78 s is long for 5 items; 3 items in ~45 s or 5 items in ~60 s holds better.
- The ordinal beat repeats identically 5x; vary the entrance (slide / pop / count-up) for items 3-5.
- No sound design visible: add soft whooshes on badges and ticks on bullets.
