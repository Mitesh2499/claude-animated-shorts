# Story analysis of the Raw sample (Krishna karma short, 40.5 s)

Private study. Source: `Raw/*.mp4`. Visual + sound structure analysed from frames, cuts, camera tracking and audio energy.
**Dialogue was NOT transcribed** (no speech-to-text installed; the energy analysis could not prove whether there is voice). Everything below is from pictures and sound dynamics. If the user supplies the transcript or a Whisper install, add a dialogue section here.

## 1. Hook (0-3 s)
Frame 1 is already the wound: a weeping child and a hunched woman on a pavement with an empty bowl, harsh yellow light. No title, no setup. Then feet walk through the frame and ignore them. Scroll-stop = visible suffering + "will anyone help?" in 2 s. The strongest element is the passers-by shown as legs only: indifference as a crowd, not a person.
## 2. Structure, pacing, transitions
Pattern: cruelty -> witness -> divine turn -> reveal -> punishment -> black. Shots 3.3 / 3.0 / 1.3 / 3.7 / 2.9 / 1.9 / 7.9 / 5 / 5 / 3.8 s. Pace is slow-slow-fast-slow, then one long awe hold, then rapid physical action. Only hard cuts; dip-to-dark once (the turn); final hard cut to black with audio tail.
## 3. Emotional triggers and relatability
Pity (child crying), anger (the kick), shame (every viewer has walked past someone), justice satisfaction (the offender is humbled), awe (cosmic form). The audience sees "ordinary people who ignore" first, so the viewer is implicated before the villain is singled out.
## 4. Conversations, expressions, pauses
The first half works through acting, not talk: held sad faces, a tear, a smoking man's smug stillness. Silence is the pause: music is absent for 16 s. Expressions escalate by swaps: smug -> grin -> gritted teeth -> broken horror face.
## 5. Suspense, curiosity loops, revelation
Loop: after the kick, a dark close-up of eyes glowing, "who is watching?" is open for ~3 s, closed by the divine reveal. A second, smaller loop: the villain is shown cool and smug, so the audience waits for him to fall. Reveal image = multiplying arms + halo (visual escalation 2 -> 8).
## 6. Cinematography, music, SFX
Push-in on every hold (1-3 %/s). Warm yellow street vs teal/white divine; pale blown-out villain world. Ambient only until 16 s, score enters at the reveal (+11 dB), contact hits as short spikes, silence between, decay over black.
## 7. Ending and shareability
Ends mid-judgement then black: no explanation, the viewer completes the moral ("karma"). Shareable because it affirms a belief the viewer wants to show others ("send to someone who treats people badly").

## WHY it works (principles to reuse)
1. **A moral wound in frame 1**: unfairness shown, not told. Viewers stay to see it resolved.
2. **Delay the face of the offender**; show his face only right before the fall so the payoff is concentrated.
3. **A silent witness beat** (quiet close-up, music out) converts pity into a need for justice.
4. **One open curiosity loop** (something is watching) closed by a larger, grander image (the reveal).
5. **Sound is a story arc**: quiet world -> sudden score = "the divine entered"; end on black so the idea lingers and the viewer shares to discuss.

## Gaps to improve in OUR scripts (the sample's weaknesses)
- No spoken closing line: add one 6-12 word quotable line (this is the main shareability upgrade).
- No relatable payoff for the victim: let the victim receive something or be seen, so the ending is emotional, not only vengeful.
- Punishment is graphic; ours is realisation/loss.
- No title or text: acceptable, but the post caption should carry the hook question.
